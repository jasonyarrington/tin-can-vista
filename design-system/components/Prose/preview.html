A walk before sunset

2026-11-09 / 4 min read

The light came in low and turned the boulders gold, and for ten minutes nothing else mattered.

Most of what I write here starts as a walk. This one began at a rusted fence on the edge of town and ended in the foothills.

Granite and prickly pear

The trail narrows between two boulders, then opens onto a patch of cactus glowing green in the last light.

Stay curious about small places.

I headed back before the shadows got long.
